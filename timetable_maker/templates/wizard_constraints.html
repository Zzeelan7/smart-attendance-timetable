Step 4 — Constraints

Configure scheduling rules. Hard constraints must be satisfied — if impossible after multiple attempts, the system will ask you to amend or remove them. Soft constraints are best-effort.

💡 If a hard constraint cannot be satisfied after 3 attempts, the result page will highlight it and ask you to amend or remove it before regenerating.
Hard Constraints Must Satisfy
Max First-Period (P1) classes per teacher per week
No teacher should have more than X classes in the first period slot of the day.
Max periods per teacher per day
A teacher cannot teach more than X periods in a single day.
No back-to-back classes for teachers
Teachers must have at least one free period between consecutive teaching slots.
Labs must be in consecutive periods
All lab sessions must occupy two adjacent time slots (e.g. P3+P4 or P5+P6).
Soft Constraints Best Effort
At least 1 third-period (P3) class per teacher per week
Try to give every teacher at least one class in the third period. Not always possible if slots are full.
Spread classes evenly across the week
Avoid stacking most classes on the same day. Distribute workload uniformly across Mon–Fri.
Teachers should have at least one free day per week
Try to leave one full day free for each teacher (for research, meetings, preparation).
